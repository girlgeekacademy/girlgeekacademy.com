In July 2024 we launched [AI High](/ai-high/), with support from the Telstra Foundation, and called on Australian high school principals to get their students involved. We also announced co-founder Lisy Kane as managing director.

The launch was covered by:

- [SmartCompany’s Neural Notes](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/exclusive/neural-notes-girl-geek-academy-ai-classroom/), 19 July 2024
- [The Educator](https://www.theeducatoronline.com/k12/news/principals-urged-to-join-ai-high/285172), 19 July 2024
- [GamesHub](https://www.gameshub.com/news/news/girl-geek-academy-ai-high-program-2643202/)
- [Women Love Tech](https://womenlovetech.com/girl-geek-academy-challenges-australian-high-school-principals-to-enrol-students-in-ai-high/)

AI High is available for 2027. [Find out how to fund it or bring it to your school](/ai-high/).
