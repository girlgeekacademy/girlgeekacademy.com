Under the Australian Curriculum, every student studies tech until the end of Year 8. But a new study of 22 Queensland schools found many let students skip Digital Technologies.

Catherine Strohfeldt reported on the research for Brisbane Times, and spoke to our CEO Sarah Moran about why inconsistent access to tech classes pushes girls out.
