Our CEO Sarah Moran spoke to Startup Daily about the national Diversity in STEM Review, and why programs that already work shouldn’t have to wait for it.

Her ask: commit funding for women in STEM programs in the May 2023 Budget, rather than waiting until the review reports in 2024.
