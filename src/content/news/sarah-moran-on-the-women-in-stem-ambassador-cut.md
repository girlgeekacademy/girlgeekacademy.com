When the federal government closed the Women in STEM Ambassador initiative, SmartCompany asked our CEO Sarah Moran what it meant for the sector.

She called it “another blow after a series of knocks to people who are trying to develop solutions to the problem”, and pointed to our Diversity in STEM Review submission, which called for publicly funded organisations to publish gender equality action plans.
