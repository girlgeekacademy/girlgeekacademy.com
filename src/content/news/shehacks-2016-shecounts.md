A women’s life doesn’t stop after children and our goal is to skill women who wish to return to the tech world and feel they may be out of touch with the fast-paced industry.

Here at [#shecounts](https://shehacks2016.slack.com/archives/shecounts), we understand that every woman matters and provides a beneficial aspect to every work place. We’re here to provide the tools to show her she can.

We have created a product that aims to facilitate women in tech transitioning back into the workforce after periods of maternity leave and raising children.

#shecounts, because she is more than a mother and she still matters.
