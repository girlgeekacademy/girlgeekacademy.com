It’s big news to hear that the SBE Accelerator is calling for women creating innovation through tech startups across Australia.

Many founders need to learn how to get their startup to succeed and it’s sometimes hard to imagine the next steps to take. Getting a solid solution to a problem you’ve noticed, making sure your solution is one that people will actually use, and designing it in a way that attracts are all parts of the startup journey.

Luckily for us, we can draw on organisations like Springboard Enterprises Australia (SBE) to support us along the way. Meet the mentors, industry experts and SBE team who work tirelessly to support at any of the upcoming info sessions. Take your startup to a new level of success.

- Melbourne: 30 January 2018 from 5pm, [click here to RSVP](https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/launch-event-melbourne-springboard-enterprises-australia-accelerator-2018-registration-42265237463)
- Brisbane: 31 January 2018 from 5pm, [click here to RSVP](https://accelerator2018laucheventbrisbane.eventbrite.com.au/)
- Sydney: 1 February 2018 from 5pm, [click here to RSVP](https://sbelaunchsyd.eventbrite.com.au/)
- Adelaide: 6 February 2018 from 5pm, [click here to RSVP](https://accelerator2018launcheventadelaide.eventbrite.com.au/)
- Perth: 7 February 2018 from 5pm, [click here to RSVP](https://accelerator2018launcheventperth.eventbrite.com.au/)

“The Springboard Accelerator program has created a real transformation in me personally. I have experienced real growth in a short space of time. I am investor ready!” – Jenny Aiken, Co-Founder & CEO of [Life Cell Marine](http://lifecellmarine.com.au/)

“Springboard has been one of the most supportive and connected networks I have been involved with. They really help women excel.” – Helen Awali, CEO of [Bookmarc](https://bookmarc.io/au)
