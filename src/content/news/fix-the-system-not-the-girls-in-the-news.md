When we lodged our submission to the national Diversity in STEM Review, our message was simple: stop asking women and girls to fit an industry that pushes them out, and invest in changing the industry instead.

The submission drew on our survey of 302 women, men and non-binary people working in tech, and set out what government could do now.

It was covered by:

- [Women’s Agenda](https://womensagenda.com.au/tech/fix-the-system-not-the-girls-girl-geek-academy-calls-for-gender-equality-investment-in-tech-industry/), 29 June 2023
- [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/technology/girl-geek-academy-calls-urgently-fix-systematic-gender-equality-issues-tech/), 30 June 2023
- [The Wire](https://www.thewire.org.au/story/time-for-action-empowering-women-in-stem/), 20 July 2023
