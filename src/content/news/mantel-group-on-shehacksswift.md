Mantel Group wrote about [SheHacksSwift](/shehacksswift/), our three-day program where high school girls and gender-diverse students learn Swift and build their own iOS apps.

The piece looks at how the program works: mentors, a crew of peers, and a “yes, and” approach that builds confidence before jumping to fix the code.

[Read the post at Mantel Group](https://community.mantelgroup.com.au/articles/post/shehacksswift---supporting-girls-learning-to-build-ios-apps-0K2QjZRNmvoYDeT)
