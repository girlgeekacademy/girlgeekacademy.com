On July 30-31 we launched #MissMakes3D – digital fabrication and 3D printing workshops for junior girl geeks! In partnership with Museum Victoria, we ran workshops with 3D Doodlers in the TEST LAB at Scienceworks

Test Lab is a temporary, pop-up maker space for older children to come and try making all types of things and be inspired by innovations made by others, including local makers and inventors. Drop in for general tinkering in the Shed, or 10-15 year olds can book into a workshop at Thursday Makers Club and Weekend Workshops
