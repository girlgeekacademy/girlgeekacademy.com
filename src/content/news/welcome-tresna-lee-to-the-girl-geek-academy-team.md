We are so excited to welcome Tresna Lee to the Girl Geek Academy team! Tresna will be developing our partner relationships as well as creating incredible experiences for women and girls at our events in 2017.

If you’re yet to experience one of her events, then keep your eyes peeled and be sure to score a ticket when we announce our first dates in March. If you’re a partner that wants to get involved in our events or help us take our mission further afield, then email partners@girlgeekacademy.com to chat with Tresna.
